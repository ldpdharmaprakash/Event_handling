About INNOVEX 2K26

Where Campus Brilliance Meets Limitless Opportunity

Welcome to a premier intra-campus symposium hosted by FACE Prep Campus at Bharathidasan College of Arts and Science. Designed to transcend traditional boundaries, this dynamic initiative invites visionary minds from all departments to converge, compete, and collaborate.

Whether you are cracking complex code, pitching groundbreaking business strategies, or captivating an audience with the power of words, this event is meticulously crafted to mirror the high-stakes demands of the modern professional world—equipping you with the ultimate head start in your career journey.
